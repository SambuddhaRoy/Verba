Troubleshooting

Shrink the overlay window EXPERIMENTAL Turn this on if a frame appears around the overlay. Tools that restyle windows, such as Windhawk's translucent‑windows mod, paint the whole window rectangle, and Verba's is a large transparent canvas. Shrinking it cuts the frame from about 90 pixels a side to under ten. To remove it completely, add Verba.exe to that tool's excluded programs.
